# Toast

- Source paths: `artifacts/wealthone-expenses/src/components/ui/toast.tsx`, `artifacts/wealthone-expenses/src/components/ui/toaster.tsx`, and `artifacts/wealthone-expenses/src/hooks/use-toast.ts`; catalog implementations: `artifacts/wealthone-design-system/src/components/ui/toast.tsx`, `artifacts/wealthone-design-system/src/components/ui/toaster.tsx`, and `artifacts/wealthone-design-system/src/hooks/use-toast.tsx`.
- Usage evidence: `src/App.tsx` mounts the application-wide `Toaster`; pages including `src/pages/advice.tsx`, `src/pages/profile.tsx`, `src/pages/budgets.tsx`, and `src/components/data-manager.tsx` call `useToast().toast` for success and destructive feedback.
- Dependencies: React, `@radix-ui/react-toast`, `class-variance-authority`, `lucide-react` (`X`), and the local `cn` utility.
- Public API: `ToastProvider`, `ToastViewport`, `Toast`, `ToastTitle`, `ToastDescription`, `ToastClose`, `ToastAction`, `ToastProps`, and `ToastActionElement`; `Toaster`; and `useToast`, `toast`, and `reducer`. `Toast` variants are `default` and `destructive`. `toast(options)` returns `{ id, dismiss, update }`; `useToast()` exposes current `toasts`, `toast`, and `dismiss`.
- Behavior: the global notification store displays one toast at a time. Toastes open with Radix swipe and entrance/exit transitions; close or Radix open-state changes dispatch dismissal and delayed removal. The toaster supplies provider, viewport, title/description/action composition, and close control. Destructive notifications apply destructive colors and action/close focus treatments.
- Story path: `artifacts/wealthone-design-system/src/preview/demos/toast.tsx` mounts `Toaster` and triggers the default notification plus a destructive notification with a retry `ToastAction`, covering the notification family, variants, action, and dismissible rendered state.
